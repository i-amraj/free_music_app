# How to Work with PK Music

Welcome to **PK Music**, a premium music streaming experience! This guide will help you master all the features of the app.

---

## 🎙️ 1. Speaking Voice Search (Mike)
We've made searching for your favorite music easier and more interactive!

*   **How to use:** Tap the **Microphone (Mike) icon** in the search bar.
*   **What happens:** 
    *   The app will speak to you and say **"Speak now"** in English.
    *   A system window will appear—just say the name of the song or artist you want to find.
    *   Once recognized, the app will announce **"Searching for [Song Name]"** and show you the results instantly.
*   **Pro Tip:** If it's your first time, make sure to click **"Allow"** when the app asks for Microphone permission.

---

## 🔔 2. Set as Ringtone
Found a song you love? You can make it your phone's ringtone with just one tap!

*   **From Search Results:** Tap the **Bell icon (🔔)** next to any song.
*   **From the Player:** Tap the **Bell icon (🔔)** at the bottom of the full-screen player.
*   **Important:** Setting a ringtone requires a special permission. 
    *   The first time you tap the bell, the app will ask you to allow "Modify System Settings".
    *   It will automatically open your phone's settings—just find **PK Music** and toggle the switch to **ON**.
    *   Go back to the app and tap the bell again. You'll see a message: **"🔔 Ringtone Set Successfully!"**

---

## 📥 3. Downloads & Offline Play
Save your favorite tracks to listen to them anytime, even without an internet connection.

*   **Downloading:** Tap the **Download icon (⬇️)** next to any song. You'll see a progress circle, followed by a **"✅ Downloaded Successfully!"** message.
*   **Accessing Downloads:** Tap the **Downloads icon** in the top right corner of the main screen.
*   **The Downloads Screen:** 
    *   This screen shows every song you've saved to your phone.
    *   Tap any song to play it immediately.
    *   **Deleting:** Want to clear space? Tap the **Trash/Delete icon** to remove a song from your device.

---

## 📤 4. Share with Friends
Share the joy of music with your friends and family!

*   **How to use:** While a song is playing, tap the **Share icon** in the player.
*   **What happens:** Your phone's share menu will open, allowing you to send the song details via WhatsApp, Telegram, or any other app.
