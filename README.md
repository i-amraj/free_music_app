# Raj Music

  <img src="logo/logo.jpeg" height="200" alt="Raj Music Logo">
</div>

**Raj Music** is a beautiful, feature-rich Material 3 YouTube Music client for Android, built with Jetpack Compose. 

## 🚀 Features & New Additions

- **Google Assistant Integration**: Play your favorite songs directly using voice commands (e.g., *"Play [Song Name] on Raj Music"*).
- **Ad-Free Experience**: Play songs from YouTube and YouTube Music without interruptions.
- **Background Playback**: Keep the music playing while using other apps.
- **Offline Mode**: Cache and download songs for offline playback.
- **Synchronized Lyrics**: Sing along with real-time lyrics and translator support.
- **Advanced Audio Controls**: Skip silence, audio normalization, and tempo/pitch adjustments.
- **Dynamic Theme & Material 3 UI**: A sleek design that adapts to your system theme.
- **Android Auto Support**: Take your music on the road.
- **Customized UI**: Enhanced layout and design elements specific to Raj Music.
